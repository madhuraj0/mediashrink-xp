MediaShrink XP Professional

Version 2.0 (Client-Side WASM Edition)

MediaShrink XP is a 100% in-browser image and video compressor that operates without server uploads. It intelligently determines bitrates and dimension scaling to hit any target size (including ultra-small targets under 8 MB, 2 MB, and 500 KB).

Features:
  • Arbitrary target size selection (KB or MB)
  • Smart adaptive resolution scaling for low bitrates
  • Native Canvas & WebP optimization for images
  • FFmpeg WebAssembly engine for MP4/WebM video
  • Batch queue with ZIP export
  • Windows XP Luna Blue Retro User Experience with Audio FX
